Colour identifies asset type. Dashed lines identify planning or design. Gardiner lines follow City road centrelines with approximate section limits; they are not work boundaries or future alignments.

Data sources

The watchlist summarizes official City project pages, reviewed 18 November 2026. Each project links its source. A source-change flag means the summary needs review, not that a new status has been confirmed.

  • City of Toronto T.O.INview — point, line and polygon project layers. Includes City divisions and wholly City-owned agencies. Excludes private developments, provincial projects, road moratoria and study-only records.
  • Gardiner strategic rehabilitation plan and Toronto road centrelines
  • Don River & Central Waterfront program
  • Open Government Licence – Toronto
  • Map: Leaflet / © OpenStreetMap contributors

Register counts represent published location records, not unique contracts or capital investments. One project can have multiple locations. The register can overlap the major watchlist. It does not cover every municipal building, park or capital program.

Register geometry comes from the City. Watchlist geometry is an illustrative point or corridor. No live traffic feed or automatic daily refresh: this is a committed snapshot refreshed with the project’s data command.
