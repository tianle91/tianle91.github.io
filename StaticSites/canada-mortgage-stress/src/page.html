How the borrowing-cost comparison works

For every month, calculate the benchmark home's principal-and-interest payment using that month's observed mortgage rate, 20% down and a fresh 25-year amortization. Canadian fixed-rate semi-annual compounding is converted to monthly payments. There is no single fixed mortgage rate applied across the history.

Both price and payment start at an index of 100 in the first shared month. For example, a payment index of 200 means twice that starting month's payment. If the payment index rises faster than the price index, borrowing costs have increased the modelled payment burden relative to price growth.

This is a modelled payment comparison, not a market valuation, inflation adjustment or income-based affordability measure. It excludes taxes, maintenance, condo fees, insurance and transaction costs. It does not model future rates at renewal or an existing owner's outstanding loan.

Definitions & data sources

“Arrears” and “delinquency” both mean 90+ days overdue here. The monthly price line breaks at the change in TRREB report coverage around 2024. The plot is limited to the period covered by both series, so the bank forecast stops at the latest available price month. Straight lines connect published observations without inventing intervening data. Rising arrears alone do not establish a house-price bottom.
