# Privacy Policy — WatchMyScore

_Last updated: 2026-06-29_

WatchMyScore is a private, on-device scoring app for your Apple Watch and iPhone.
This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## No account, no cloud, no tracking

WatchMyScore has no user accounts and no backend server. It does not collect,
transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no analytics or advertising
SDK.

## Your scores stay on your devices

Match scoring, workout sessions, history, and any video overlays run and are
stored entirely on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Your watch and iPhone sync this
data directly to each other over Apple's device-to-device connection so both can
show the same information. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

## Apple system services

- **Health & workouts** use Apple HealthKit (with your permission) to record
  workout sessions so your activity appears in the Health app. This data stays on
  your device under your control and is never sent to anyone. It is never used for
  advertising or shared with third parties.

## Purchases

The optional **WatchMyScore Pro** upgrade is processed by Apple via StoreKit.
Apple handles all payment information and billing; WatchMyScore never sees your
payment details. The app only reads Apple's entitlement status to unlock
unlimited use. Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled and can be managed or
cancelled in your Apple ID settings. Subscriptions are also subject to the
[Apple Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).
