# Privacy Policy — Dedup2

_Last updated: 2026-06-09_

Dedup2 is a private, on-device photo utility. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with your data.

## No account, no cloud, no tracking

Dedup2 has no user accounts and no backend server. It does not collect, transmit,
sell, or share any personal data. There is no analytics or advertising SDK.

## Your photos stay on your device

Duplicate detection, quality scoring, and compression run entirely on your iPhone
using Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision, PhotoKit, Core Image). Your images are
never uploaded anywhere.

## Apple system services

- **Location backfill** uses Apple's reverse geocoding (`CLGeocoder`) and `MapKit`
  to show place names and map previews. This sends a photo's **coordinates**
  (never the image itself) to Apple, exactly as the built-in Photos app does.

## Purchases

The optional **Dedup2 Pro** subscription is processed by Apple via StoreKit. Apple
handles all payment information and billing; Dedup2 never sees your payment details.
The app only reads Apple's entitlement status to unlock unlimited use. Subscriptions
auto-renew until cancelled and can be managed or cancelled in your Apple ID settings.
Subscriptions are also subject to the
[Apple Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).
