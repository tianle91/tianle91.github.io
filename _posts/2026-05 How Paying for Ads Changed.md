Ads used to be pay-for-placement where advertisers pay for spots on publications.
When ads moved online, what changed is personalization, where we can show different ads to different users for the same spot.
But the pricing model didn't change until PPC (pay-per-click) started gaining momentum in the 2000s, with paying for outcomes being the new trend, promising to align cost with value.
So how do they do it and is it actually better?
