# How to Have Your Privacy Cake and Eat It Too

## 1. Introduction: Is Google a True Privacy Advocate?
* **Core Premise**: Examining the tension between Google's outward public stance as a privacy defender and its core commercial business model.
* **Historical Context**: Analysing the origins and development of the **Google FLEDGE** (Privacy Sandbox) proposals.

## 2. Consumer Representation vs. Platform Control
* **The Extension Gap**: Is Google genuinely pro-consumer? 
  * *Evidence*: The persistent lack of browser extensions on mobile Google Chrome since Chrome and Android emerged, compared to mobile Safari and Firefox which allow extensions and thus mobile ad blockers.
* **Geopolitical & Technological Dominance**: To what extent does Google control the advertising rails? Does Google possess an unfair, structural advantage in online advertising?
  * *Investigation*: Would the implementation of the FLEDGE proposal have worsened this monopoly?

## 3. The Antitrust Paradox
* **Unrepresented Parties**: The core antitrust cases are framed as *Google vs. Competitor Ad Tech Companies*, but the primary stakeholder—the end user's direct interest and data representation—has no active representation in court.
* **Alternate Pathways**: What could a user-aligned solution look like? Creating a neutral framework enabling fair market competition while mathematically guaranteeing privacy.
* **Precedents**: Using Google's court-ordered search data sharing practices in related antitrust cases as a preview of how this new, highly regulated advertising marketplace might look.
