# Privacy Policy — WorkoutTally

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

WorkoutTally is a private, on-device workout tracker for iPhone. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## No account, no cloud, no tracking

WorkoutTally has no user accounts and no backend server. It does not collect,
transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no analytics or advertising
SDK, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

## Your workouts stay on your iPhone

Your workouts, exercises, sets, templates, and settings are stored entirely on
your iPhone, in the app's own storage. None of it is uploaded anywhere. Deleting
the app removes that data from your device.

You can export your own data at any time — as a CSV of every logged set, or as a
full JSON backup — using the export controls in Settings. Those exports go
wherever *you* send them through the iOS share sheet; the app itself never sends
them anywhere.

## Apple system services

- **Notifications** are used only to alert you when a rest timer finishes while
  the app is in the background or your screen is locked. They are scheduled and
  delivered locally on your device — nothing is sent to a server, and the app asks
  your permission before scheduling any.

## Purchases

The optional **WorkoutTally Pro** upgrade is processed by Apple via StoreKit.
Apple handles all payment information and billing; WorkoutTally never sees your
payment details. The app only reads Apple's entitlement status to unlock
unlimited templates. Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled and can be managed
or cancelled in your Apple ID settings. Subscriptions are also subject to the
[Apple Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).
