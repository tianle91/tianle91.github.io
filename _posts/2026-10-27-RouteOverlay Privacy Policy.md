# RouteOverlay Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

RouteOverlay is provided by Tianle Chen. It combines existing Apple Health
workouts with photos and videos to create route overlays, organize workout media,
review media locations and export routes. This policy explains how information is
used when you use RouteOverlay and contact us for support.

## Your workouts and media

RouteOverlay processes workout and media information on your device. It has no
app account or developer-operated backend for uploading workouts or media. It does
not include advertising, tracking or an analytics SDK. We do not sell your Health
information or use it for advertising or marketing profiles.

With your permission, RouteOverlay reads existing Health workouts, recorded GPS
routes, walking/running and cycling distance, active energy and heart rate when
available. It uses this information to display workouts, match media by capture
time, suggest locations, create maps and metrics, and export GPX files. It does
not record workouts or write information to Health.

Photos access lets RouteOverlay read photos, videos, capture dates, locations and
other media information needed for matching and editing. It can save exported
copies, link or create albums, and change album membership or media locations
after you review and confirm the changes. Album sync changes membership without
deleting the original items from Photos. A location save changes the selected
item's location metadata in Photos; deleting RouteOverlay does not undo it.

Limited Photos access restricts the items available to the app. Full access is
required for album comparison and sync. If an original is stored in iCloud Photos,
Apple may download it to your device. You can control automatic original downloads
in RouteOverlay Settings: Off, Wi-Fi only, or Always, including cellular. Manual
downloads and cancellation are available when supported by the media flow.

## Apple services and optional sharing

RouteOverlay uses Apple's HealthKit, Photos, MapKit, geocoding and StoreKit
services. Map and place-name requests may send coordinates and related requests
to Apple. iCloud downloads and purchases also communicate with Apple. Apple's
services are subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
RouteOverlay should not be treated as an entirely offline app.

When you export or share a photo, video or GPX file, it may contain precise routes,
timestamps, locations, elevation, heart rate and other information you choose to
include. Review the output before sharing it. Your chosen destination app or
service handles the shared information under its own practices.

Optional handoffs to Dedup2 or OfflineClipper pass the selected Photos identifiers
or album context to the app you choose. Those apps are not needed for core
RouteOverlay features. RouteOverlay does not send your workouts or media to
developer servers as part of these features.

## Purchases

Apple processes monthly subscriptions and lifetime purchases. RouteOverlay reads
verified purchase and subscription status to determine Pro access; we do not
receive your payment-card details. Restore Purchases asks Apple to synchronize
your purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.
Deleting RouteOverlay does not cancel an Apple subscription.

## Information stored on your device

RouteOverlay stores local app information such as cached workouts, media
references, export history, free workout unlocks, album links, editor preferences,
language, download preferences and cached place names. This information supports
the app's features and remains until it is updated, cleared by an available app
control or removed with the app's local data. Device backup and restore may also
be managed by your operating system and Apple settings.

You can clear the place-name cache in RouteOverlay Settings. To remove the app's
local data, delete the app rather than offloading it. This also removes local
preferences and workout-unlock history; it does not delete original Health data,
original Photos items, exported files or saved Photos edits. Manage those items
separately in Health, Photos, Files or the destination where you saved them.
Information already shared with another app or person is controlled separately.

## Your choices and requests

You can revoke Health and Photos access through iOS/iPadOS Settings and Health,
and change the Photos items available under limited access. Revocation prevents
future access to the affected information; it does not automatically remove
previously saved exports or undo Photos changes.

No RouteOverlay account exists to delete. We cannot remotely access or erase the
Health/Photos libraries or app-local records on your device. For questions about
your privacy choices or deletion of information you sent us directly, contact
[tianlechen@gmail.com](mailto:tianlechen@gmail.com) or use http://tianle91.com.

## Support messages and this website

If you contact us, we receive the contact information, message and attachments
you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue.
Please avoid sending Health records, precise routes, private media, passwords,
payment details or Apple Account credentials. If a sample is needed, use a
non-sensitive example and remove identifying information.

Support messages may be handled by our email/support provider. We keep them only
as needed to address the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable
legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of support information;
any retention that is still required will be explained in our response.

Visiting tianle91.com is separate from using the RouteOverlay app. The website is
hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process IP addresses and other
connection information to serve and secure pages. See the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The website includes Google Analytics code. When you visit website pages, your
browser may send page URLs, IP addresses and browser information to Google, and
analytics may use cookies. This website behavior is separate from the app, which
has no analytics SDK. See
[how Google uses information from sites using its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
for details and available controls. You can also manage cookies in your browser.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Google processes email under its
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Do not include private
workout or media data in an initial support request.
